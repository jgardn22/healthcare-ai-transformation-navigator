About these sources

Cowork library cards come from the Microsoft Copilot Cowork Healthcare Use-Case Library. Business Apps catalog cards come from the Microsoft Business Applications Pre-Built Agents and Capabilities report; they cover first-party Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Copilot Studio capabilities mapped to healthcare lines of business, and preview features may change. Dragon Copilot cards are drawn from Microsoft Learn documentation. Copilot Studio agent use cases and named agents come from the AI Apps and Agents for Healthcare Providers and Payers Executive Guide. Every agent use case in the Executive Guide is listed under Copilot Studio; each card’s footer shows the guide’s likely build, and many are Power App builds that a Copilot Studio agent can front. Impact tags use the six value levers from the Executive Guide, which line up with HAVEN's value lines. Named agents carry the guide's own tags; other tags are inferred from the use case text. Lateetud cards come from a partner solution shelf at field.lateetud.com; they are vendor-described, not Microsoft products, and should be validated before customer use. Clinical and nursing cards also draw on a nurse Build-A-Thon customer story, the Nursing Operations Assist multi-agent team, and a nursing capacity executive brief. Cards marked "Navigator addition" were drafted for the PMO line of business from the PMO Reimagined session and the Executive Guide, and should be validated before customer use. Supply chain cards marked “Supply chain webinar” were drafted from the October 1, 2026 Supply Chain Modernization session and its demo; they are illustrative and should be validated before customer use. Cards marked “Cowork + Copilot Studio provider portfolio” were drafted from an October 8, 2026 portfolio report for U.S. healthcare providers; the report’s statistics are not reproduced here, and the cards should be validated before customer use. Engineering team cards (Copilot Studio IDs starting EN-, and Cowork cards marked “engineering team scenario”) are scenarios built on Microsoft Learn documentation for the Azure DevOps MCP Server and Copilot Studio; they are illustrative, not product commitments, and should be validated before customer use. Pattern 5 cards describe Microsoft developer tools, drawn from Microsoft Learn documentation and Microsoft developer-tool positioning; the custom solution scenarios (IDs starting PC-) are illustrative designs, not product commitments, and should be validated before customer use. Illustrative overviews are conversation starters, not Microsoft product commitments.

The big idea

AI is not a single product. It is a flow from assistants, to delegation, to agentic teams, to intelligence inside the applications you already use, to custom solutions your own developers build.

1

Five patterns, one evolution

Each pattern changes who does the work, from AI beside one person to AI built into the systems of record, and to AI your own developers build.

    2

    The operating model shifts toward people

    The right pattern moves your people to the high-value work that needs real expertise, and gives AI the low-value work that still needs coordination.

    3

    Agentic teams make it repeatable

    One assist agent is the front door. Specialist agents work across your business systems, and humans stay in the loop for approvals and exceptions.
